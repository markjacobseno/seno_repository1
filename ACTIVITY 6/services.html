Your cup, your way

Start with a flavor feeling.

Explore a few general ideas for putting together a cup that sounds good to you. Mix and match what’s available where you order.

01 The starting point

Choose a tea mood

Go for familiar and cozy, light and mellow, or a more fragrant tea note. Start with the flavor you feel like enjoying.

02 A little texture

Think about the sip

Prefer a smooth drink, or a little chew with each sip? Texture can change the feel of a cup just as much as its flavor.

03 Make it yours

Find your balance

Consider how sweet or rich you’d like your drink, and ask about the options available when you order.

These are flavor ideas, not a confirmed menu or service list. Ingredients, choices, and availability depend on the shop you visit.

Keep exploring
